Multi-Omics Chordoma Analysis

venteicherlab.umn.edu →

Lead computational analyst, Venteicher Lab

  • snRNA-seq
  • Bulk RNA-seq
  • Epigenomics
  • Spatial Transcriptomics
  • NMF
  • HPC

Performed single nucleus RNA sequencing on primary chordoma tumors across four molecular subgroups. Built pipelines integrating Cell Ranger, Harmony, and NMF to define recurrent malignant transcriptional programs, identify key regulators, and characterize intra- and inter-tumoral heterogeneity — a rare cancer with few recurrent coding mutations where standard gene-based analysis fails.

First application of meta-program analysis in chordoma; delivered a full catalog of malignant transcriptional programs across subgroups with clinical correlation.

Developmental Origin of Chordoma

Computational co-investigator, Venteicher Lab

  • Cross-species
  • snRNA-seq
  • scVI
  • foundation model
  • RNA velocity
  • Mouse notochord E8.5–E11.5

Built a cross-species atlas integrating mouse notochord snRNA-seq (stages E8.5–E11.5) with human chordoma tumor data using Harmony, scVI transfer learning, and dynamic time warping. Fine-tuned a foundation model to embed genes and transcriptional programs in a shared vector space, enabling cosine similarity searches across species to identify conserved regulators that persist from embryonic development into malignancy.

First cross-species developmental atlas of chordoma; nominated conserved notochord regulators (including TBXT/Brachyury) active across tumor subgroups.

Clinical Outcomes in Hematopoietic Transplantation

Blood Advances →

First author · Masonic Cancer Center

  • First author
  • Published
  • n=1,077
  • Blood Advances
  • ASH Award

Led retrospective analysis of 1,077 adult transplant recipients treated at the University of Minnesota (2000–2016), comparing 5-year healthcare burden across graft sources. Demonstrated that umbilical cord blood transplant recipients had significantly lower long-term healthcare utilization versus marrow or peripheral blood grafts.

First-author peer-reviewed publication in Blood Advances (2021). Oral presentation and first-author abstract at the 60th ASH Annual Meeting, San Diego — received the Outstanding Abstract Achievement Award.
