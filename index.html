How efficiently does an AI cluster actually run?

I built an open framework that turns cluster telemetry into a deterministic efficiency score. Seven engines account for factors including GPU utilization, thermal efficiency, carbon, scheduler policy, and hardware fit.

Live telemetry→7 engines→One score

Published weights. Independently reproducible results. Validated against public traces from MIT Supercloud, Alibaba Helios, and Microsoft Philly.
